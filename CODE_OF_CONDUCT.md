# Code of conduct

Domovyk is a small project that welcomes everyone: beginners and experts, Linux
veterans and people who just want their webcam to work.

## Our standards

- Be kind, patient and respectful, in issues, pull requests, reviews and
  discussions.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback on the work, never on the person. Explain why.
- Welcome questions. Every expert was once a beginner.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's logs, machine details or personal
  data.

Not acceptable:
- harassment, insults or personal attacks;
- discriminatory jokes or language;
- sexualized content;
- publishing others' private information;
- sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code, and may
block people who repeatedly or seriously break it. To report a problem, contact
the maintainers privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/digitwolf/domovyk/security/advisories/new)
marked "conduct", or through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept
confidential.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
